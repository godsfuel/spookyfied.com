Zero-Retention Privacy Model

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Architecture Overview

SpookyFied.com operates on a strict sovereign, peer-to-peer (P2P) routing architecture. Unlike conventional cloud distribution networks, our infrastructure maintains a zero-retention data standard. No media files, encrypted messages, emails, or video streams are stored on central cloud servers.

2. Data Handling

All transfers route directly from node to node utilizing cryptographic handshakes anchored to your hardware Spooky Key. Once a data packet, file transfer, or direct video stream concludes, zero telemetry or persistent cache is retained by intermediary routers.

3. Compliance & Sovereignty

You maintain absolute ownership and control over your data payloads, local vaults, and peer routing tables.
