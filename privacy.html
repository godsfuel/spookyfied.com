Zero-Retention Privacy Policy

Project Spooky operates on a strict zero-knowledge, stateless infrastructure. Because all cryptographic anchoring, key generation, and packet deconstruction occur locally via your hardware anchor, our servers hold zero user logs, telemetry records, or transmission histories.

Data Handling & Storage

We do not store your personal files, video conference streams, or recipient metadata. Once a transmission session closes, all transitional pathways are instantly flushed from memory.
