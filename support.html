How do I start my local node automatically?

Your local device runs securely via background services. The system handles background execution automatically upon login to ensure your node is always ready.

How does vendor redirection work?

When redirected from an authorized vendor storefront, the system passes a secure token through our gateway, instantly authorizing your session and linking your device's unique hardware key with complete privacy.
