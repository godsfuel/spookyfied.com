How do I start my local node automatically?

Your node runs via `spooky_controller.py`. Use the automated `spooky_start.bat` script placed in your Windows startup folder for seamless background operation upon login.

How does vendor order redirection work?

When redirected from an authorized storefront (such as Bounce Airbags), the engine passes an HMAC-signed token through `/api/generate-key`, instantly authorizing your session and returning you with full access.
